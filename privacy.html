Momentum AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Momentum AI is a personal desktop app built and used by Ethan Weller. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What data it accesses

With your permission, Momentum AI uses the Gmail read-only scope (gmail.readonly) to read message headers and content so it can find internship, co-op, recruiting, and course-related emails. It never sends, deletes, labels, or changes email.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is processed and stored locally on the user's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server run by Momentum AI.
  • To classify a relevant email, the app may send that email's text, with personal contact details removed, to an AI service (xAI's Grok API). That service is used only to sort and summarize the email for the user.
  • Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • Humans do not read your email, except for the user on their own computer.

Limited Use

Momentum AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect Gmail in Momentum AI's Settings and choose to delete stored email data. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
