Momentum AI

Momentum AI is a personal desktop study coach built and used by Ethan Weller, a student at Georgia Tech. It helps one student plan coursework, track deadlines, prepare for exams, and keep up with internship and co-op opportunities.

Gmail access

With the user's permission, Momentum AI reads (read-only) the user's own Gmail to find internship, co-op, recruiting, and course-related emails and show them inside the app. It never sends, deletes, or changes email.

This is a personal, non-commercial app. It is not offered to the public.
